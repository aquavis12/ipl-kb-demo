Title: Indian Premier League - season results 2008 to 2026
Last updated: 2026-08-12
Authority: Reference document for this knowledge base demo.

The Indian Premier League is a men's Twenty20 franchise cricket league in India.
The first season was played in 2008. The 2026 season was the nineteenth edition.
Eight different franchises have won the title across those nineteen seasons.

## Season by season winners and runners-up

2008: Rajasthan Royals beat Chennai Super Kings. Winning captain Shane Warne.
2009: Deccan Chargers beat Royal Challengers Bangalore. Winning captain Adam Gilchrist.
2010: Chennai Super Kings beat Mumbai Indians. Winning captain MS Dhoni.
2011: Chennai Super Kings beat Royal Challengers Bangalore. Winning captain MS Dhoni.
2012: Kolkata Knight Riders beat Chennai Super Kings. Winning captain Gautam Gambhir.
2013: Mumbai Indians beat Chennai Super Kings. Winning captain Rohit Sharma.
2014: Kolkata Knight Riders beat Kings XI Punjab. Winning captain Gautam Gambhir.
2015: Mumbai Indians beat Chennai Super Kings. Winning captain Rohit Sharma.
2016: Sunrisers Hyderabad beat Royal Challengers Bangalore. Winning captain David Warner.
2017: Mumbai Indians beat Rising Pune Supergiant. Winning captain Rohit Sharma.
2018: Chennai Super Kings beat Sunrisers Hyderabad. Winning captain MS Dhoni.
2019: Mumbai Indians beat Chennai Super Kings. Winning captain Rohit Sharma.
2020: Mumbai Indians beat Delhi Capitals. Winning captain Rohit Sharma.
2021: Chennai Super Kings beat Kolkata Knight Riders. Winning captain MS Dhoni.
2022: Gujarat Titans beat Rajasthan Royals. Winning captain Hardik Pandya.
2023: Chennai Super Kings beat Gujarat Titans. Winning captain MS Dhoni.
2024: Kolkata Knight Riders beat Sunrisers Hyderabad. Winning captain Shreyas Iyer.
2025: Royal Challengers Bengaluru beat Punjab Kings. Winning captain Rajat Patidar.
2026: Royal Challengers Bengaluru beat Gujarat Titans. Winning captain Rajat Patidar.

## Titles won by franchise

Mumbai Indians have won five titles, in 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020.
Chennai Super Kings have won five titles, in 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021 and 2023.
Kolkata Knight Riders have won three titles, in 2012, 2014 and 2024.
Royal Challengers Bengaluru have won two titles, in 2025 and 2026.
Rajasthan Royals have won one title, in 2008.
Deccan Chargers have won one title, in 2009. That franchise no longer exists.
Sunrisers Hyderabad have won one title, in 2016.
Gujarat Titans have won one title, in 2022.

Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings are the joint most successful franchises with
five titles each.

## The 2025 season

Royal Challengers Bengaluru won their first IPL title in 2025.
It came in their eighteenth season in the league.
They beat Punjab Kings by six runs in the final, played on 3 June 2025.
Punjab Kings have never won the IPL and have lost two finals, in 2014 and 2025.

## The 2026 season

The 2026 season was the nineteenth edition of the IPL.
The season began on 28 March 2026 and concluded on 31 May 2026.
Royal Challengers Bengaluru won the 2026 title, successfully defending their 2025 title.
They beat Gujarat Titans by five wickets in the final.
The final was played on 31 May 2026 at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad.
The winning captain was Rajat Patidar.
Royal Challengers Bengaluru became the third franchise to win back-to-back IPL titles,
after Chennai Super Kings and Mumbai Indians.
This was Royal Challengers Bengaluru's second IPL title in total.
Gujarat Titans have now lost one final, in 2026, and won one final, in 2022.

## Teams that have never won

Punjab Kings have never won the IPL.
Delhi Capitals have never won the IPL, including under their earlier name Delhi Daredevils.
Lucknow Super Giants have never won the IPL.
Rising Pune Supergiant reached the 2017 final but the franchise existed for only two
seasons, 2016 and 2017.
No IPL final has ever been decided by a Super Over.
