Title: Indian Premier League - franchises and home grounds
Last updated: 2026-08-12
Authority: Reference document for this knowledge base demo.

Ten franchises competed in the 2026 Indian Premier League season.

## Mumbai Indians

Mumbai Indians are based in Mumbai, Maharashtra.
Their home ground is the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai.
They joined the league in the inaugural 2008 season.
They have won five IPL titles, the joint most alongside Chennai Super Kings.
Their title-winning captain across all five was Rohit Sharma.
Their team colours are blue and gold.

## Chennai Super Kings

Chennai Super Kings are based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
Their home ground is the M. A. Chidambaram Stadium, commonly called Chepauk.
They joined the league in the inaugural 2008 season.
They have won five IPL titles, the joint most alongside Mumbai Indians.
Their title-winning captain across all five was MS Dhoni.
They did not play in the 2016 and 2017 seasons due to a two-year suspension.
Their team colour is yellow.

## Royal Challengers Bengaluru

Royal Challengers Bengaluru are based in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
Their home ground is the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru.
They joined the league in the inaugural 2008 season.
They were known as Royal Challengers Bangalore until the name was updated to Royal
Challengers Bengaluru.
They have won two IPL titles, in 2025 and 2026, both under captain Rajat Patidar.
They lost three finals before their first title, in 2009, 2011 and 2016.
Their team colours are red and black.

## Kolkata Knight Riders

Kolkata Knight Riders are based in Kolkata, West Bengal.
Their home ground is Eden Gardens in Kolkata.
They joined the league in the inaugural 2008 season.
They have won three IPL titles, in 2012, 2014 and 2024.
Gautam Gambhir captained the 2012 and 2014 titles. Shreyas Iyer captained the 2024 title.
Their team colours are purple and gold.

## Sunrisers Hyderabad

Sunrisers Hyderabad are based in Hyderabad, Telangana.
Their home ground is the Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium in Hyderabad.
They entered the league in 2013, replacing the Deccan Chargers franchise.
They have won one IPL title, in 2016, under captain David Warner.
They lost finals in 2018 and 2024.
Their team colours are orange and black.

## Rajasthan Royals

Rajasthan Royals are based in Jaipur, Rajasthan.
Their home ground is the Sawai Mansingh Stadium in Jaipur.
They joined the league in the inaugural 2008 season.
They won the very first IPL title in 2008 under captain Shane Warne.
They lost the 2022 final to Gujarat Titans.
They did not play in the 2016 and 2017 seasons due to a two-year suspension.
Their team colour is pink.

## Delhi Capitals

Delhi Capitals are based in Delhi.
Their home ground is the Arun Jaitley Stadium in Delhi.
They joined the league in the inaugural 2008 season as the Delhi Daredevils.
The franchise was renamed Delhi Capitals in 2019.
They have never won an IPL title.
They reached their only final in 2020 and lost to Mumbai Indians.
Their team colours are blue and red.

## Punjab Kings

Punjab Kings are based in Punjab and play home matches in Mohali and Dharamsala.
Their primary home ground is the Maharaja Yadavindra Singh Stadium in Mullanpur, Mohali.
They joined the league in the inaugural 2008 season as Kings XI Punjab.
The franchise was renamed Punjab Kings in 2021.
They have never won an IPL title.
They have lost two finals, in 2014 and 2025.
Their team colours are red and silver.

## Gujarat Titans

Gujarat Titans are based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
Their home ground is the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad.
They entered the league in 2022 as an expansion franchise.
They won the IPL title in their first season, 2022, under captain Hardik Pandya.
They lost the 2023 final to Chennai Super Kings and the 2026 final to Royal
Challengers Bengaluru.
Their team colours are navy blue and gold.

## Lucknow Super Giants

Lucknow Super Giants are based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.
Their home ground is the Bharat Ratna Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee Ekana Cricket Stadium.
They entered the league in 2022 as an expansion franchise alongside Gujarat Titans.
They have never won an IPL title and have never reached a final.
Their team colours are teal and gold.

## Former franchises

Deccan Chargers were based in Hyderabad and won the 2009 title. The franchise was
terminated in 2012 and replaced by Sunrisers Hyderabad.
Pune Warriors India played from 2011 to 2013.
Kochi Tuskers Kerala played only the 2011 season.
Gujarat Lions played the 2016 and 2017 seasons.
Rising Pune Supergiant played the 2016 and 2017 seasons and reached the 2017 final.
